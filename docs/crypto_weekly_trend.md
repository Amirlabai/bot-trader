# Crypto book lock

Daily golden cross. Trend is the last completed weekly candle (week ends Sunday): weekly close versus weekly EMA 150. The open week is not used.

One six-pack for every crypto pair: fast 10, slow 20, weekly EMA 150, ATR 14, stop 1, trail 3. ADX and volume gates are off. The trail starts at entry.

Chosen from the two-year shared-wallet search after ZEC and XRP were left out of the fit. Those pairs still trade. The same six-pack is on every crypto wallet. Risk is the fraction of equity lost if the stop fills. The original four wallets are 1%. The same four styles also run at 2%, 3%, 5%, 10%, and 15%. Above 1%, a position may use the whole wallet so the stop risk is not cut by the 25% notional cap. Cash still caps size. The styles are:

1. Long, asset trail
2. Long, TP1 then trail
3. Long/Short, asset trail
4. Long/Short, TP1 then trail

Shorts use the daily death cross while the last completed week is below the weekly EMA.
