Playback pauses on every entry and exit. Press Continue to resume. Check Don't pause to run through trades without stopping. Check Skip replay to show the finished chart as soon as the sim loads. Mid-slope presets enter when the high-low midpoint crosses above the open-close midpoint, both slopes meet the Min floor, and close is above the trend EMA. Min 0 means the slope only has to be positive. After an exit, both slopes must go negative and then both positive before another entry. Falling under Min is not enough. Close-slope presets drop those averages: entry is a close slope at or above Min, and close above the trend EMA. After an exit, that slope must go negative and then positive before another entry. SL line is the stop used for the wick check (prior bar). Trail updates from close after that check. Ambiguous bars (wick between old and new trail) are rechecked on a finer TF (4h→1h, daily→4h).
